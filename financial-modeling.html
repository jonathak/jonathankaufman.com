Financial Modeling

Financial models are mathematical representations of businesses, financial instruments, and economic relationships. They are used to estimate value, forecast financial performance, evaluate alternatives, and understand the consequences of uncertainty.

My work in financial modeling combines corporate finance, financial accounting, applied mathematics, and computational methods.

Topics

Valuation

Methods for estimating the value of businesses, assets, and financial securities, including discounted cash flow analysis, comparable company analysis, and the valuation of early-stage companies.

Financial Analysis & Forecasting

Analysis and modeling of financial statements, operating performance, cash flows, and financing requirements. Topics include financial accounting, integrated three-statement models, budgeting, forecasting, scenario analysis, and long-term financial planning.

Capital Structure & Securities

Analysis of equity, debt, convertible securities, warrants, and options. Topics include capitalization tables, dilution, liquidation preferences, and the allocation of value among different classes of securities.

Simulation & Quantitative Methods

Mathematical and computational approaches to financial problems involving uncertainty or complex relationships. Topics include Monte Carlo simulation, probability distributions, option pricing, sensitivity analysis, and numerical optimization.

Practical Applications

Startup financing

How much capital will a company require, when will it be needed, and how will alternative financing structures affect ownership?

Mergers & acquisitions

What is a business worth, and how do different transaction structures affect the economics for buyers and sellers?

Biotechnology development

How do development costs, clinical timelines, regulatory probabilities, and potential revenues affect the financial outlook and valuation of a therapeutic program?

Equity compensation

How should private-company common stock, employee options, and other equity-linked instruments be valued?

Financial restructuring

How do alternative restructuring scenarios affect enterprise value, liquidity, and recoveries for creditors and shareholders?

Business planning

How do changes in operating assumptions, financing arrangements, and market conditions affect financial performance and cash requirements?

Experience

My financial modeling experience spans executive financial management, investment banking, technology commercialization, and corporate transactions.

I have developed and evaluated models involving operating forecasts, business valuation, capital formation, securities valuation, transaction analysis, and financial restructuring, particularly for biotechnology and emerging-technology businesses.
