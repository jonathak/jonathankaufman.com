Corporate Finance

Corporate finance concerns the decisions through which businesses obtain capital, invest resources, undertake transactions, and manage financial obligations. These decisions affect not only a company's financial position but also the interests of its shareholders, creditors, employees, and other stakeholders.

Sound financial decisions require an understanding of both economics and implementation: the terms of a financing, the structure of a transaction, the timing of cash flows, and the legal and organizational constraints under which a business operates.

Topics

Capital Raising & Financing

Decisions about when and how to raise capital, including equity financing, debt, convertible instruments, and other funding arrangements. Financing terms influence liquidity, ownership, financial flexibility, and the allocation of risk among investors.

Mergers, Acquisitions & Transactions

Financial and structural considerations in acquisitions, divestitures, and business combinations. Topics include transaction alternatives, due diligence, purchase price arrangements, financing, and the distribution of proceeds.

Corporate Development & Strategy

Evaluation of opportunities to build, acquire, license, partner, or divest. Strategic decisions often require comparing alternatives with different capital requirements, execution risks, timelines, and potential economic outcomes.

Financial Restructuring

Responses to financial distress, including liquidity management, negotiations with creditors, recapitalizations, asset sales, and formal restructuring processes. The available alternatives depend on the company's obligations, assets, and prospects.

Corporate Governance & Financial Management

Financial oversight and decision-making within a business, including budgeting, capital allocation, financial reporting, internal controls, and the roles of management and the board in evaluating significant commitments.

Practical Applications

Financing an early-stage company

How much capital should be raised, what milestones should it support, and how do financing terms affect existing and new investors?

Evaluating a potential acquisition

What economic benefits justify a transaction, what obligations and risks accompany the assets, and how should consideration be structured?

Commercializing technology

Should a business develop technology internally, license it to a partner, form a joint venture, or pursue another route to market?

Managing a liquidity shortfall

What options are available when expected cash resources are insufficient to meet obligations, and how do those options affect stakeholders?

Making capital allocation decisions

How should management compare competing investments and commitments when capital, time, and organizational capacity are limited?
