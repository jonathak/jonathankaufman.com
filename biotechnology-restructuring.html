Biotechnology Asset Assessment, Preservation & Disposition

Specialized scientific, regulatory, intellectual property, and operational support for distressed biotechnology companies, Chapter 11 proceedings, assignments for the benefit of creditors (ABCs), restructuring advisors, and prospective asset purchasers. This work draws on direct experience preserving, evaluating, selling, and transferring biotechnology assets during a Chapter 11 restructuring.

Initial Asset Assessment

A focused assessment of biotechnology assets, including:

  • Inventory of patents, INDs, clinical data, manufacturing records, and development programs.
  • Identification of critical patent, regulatory, and vendor obligations.
  • Estimated 13-week asset-preservation budget.
  • Identification of documentation gaps and transaction-readiness issues.
  • Written recommendations and prioritized actions.

Additional Services

  • Patent and Regulatory Continuity. Coordination of patent prosecution, FDA reporting, IND maintenance, and other time-sensitive requirements.
  • Technical Due Diligence. Organization and evaluation of scientific, clinical, regulatory, and manufacturing records for prospective buyers.
  • Asset Marketing and Buyer Diligence Support. Preparation of technical asset summaries and marketing materials; coordination of scientific, clinical, regulatory, and intellectual property information; and responses to prospective buyers' technical due diligence questions.
  • Asset Preservation and Transfer. Coordination of essential vendors, clinical materials, development records, and regulatory sponsorship transfers.
