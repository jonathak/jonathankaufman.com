Biotechnology Restructuring & Asset Advisory

Biotechnology assets can lose substantial value during a restructuring or wind-down if patent and regulatory deadlines are missed, essential vendor services or materials are not maintained, or the costs of preserving these assets are omitted from cash budgets. Conversely, maintaining regulatory and intellectual property continuity, documenting the status of development programs, and organizing clinical, CMC, and other technical records can make assets easier to evaluate and transfer. A well-structured data room and timely responses to diligence questions can reduce uncertainty and transaction delays, help retain prospective buyers, and improve the prospects for a competitive sale and greater recoveries for the estate.

  • Asset assessment and disposition. Inventorying and evaluating therapeutic programs, patents, regulatory filings, clinical data, manufacturing information, and related assets; supporting sale processes and technology transfers.
  • Patent and regulatory continuity. Tracking patent prosecution deadlines, IND annual reports and other FDA obligations, and coordinating necessary work with patent counsel and regulatory specialists.
  • Due diligence and data rooms. Organizing clinical study reports, FDA correspondence, CMC documentation, patent portfolios, contracts, and other records; responding to technical questions from prospective buyers.
  • Wind-down operations. Reviewing and maintaining essential vendor arrangements, material storage, regulatory publishing services, databases, and other resources needed to preserve asset value while staff and budgets are reduced.
  • Financial and transaction support. Preparing budgets and cash forecasts, evaluating sale alternatives, and coordinating financial information with management, counsel, and restructuring advisors.
