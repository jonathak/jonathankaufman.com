Biotechnology Restructuring & Asset Advisory

My mission is to maximize the value recovered from biotechnology assets in distressed situations. I work to quickly understand the assets from scientific, clinical, regulatory, intellectual property, and financial perspectives, identify what matters most, and address what may be missing or at risk. I focus on preserving value, maintaining continuity, and improving the prospects for a successful transaction. When management resources are limited, I can personally undertake much of the technical, regulatory, financial, and operational work required.

I bring extensive education in science and finance, together with 25 years of hands-on experience in technology and intellectual property assessment, preclinical and clinical development, manufacturing, clinical data analysis, and FDA regulatory affairs. My experience includes FDA designation and meeting requests, IND sponsorship transfers, patent prosecution and Office Action responses, preservation of clinical materials, technology transfer and licensing, corporate acquisitions, asset sales, and financial and recovery analysis.

My work is directed toward making biotechnology assets easier to evaluate, preserve, and acquire. I identify critical deadlines and continuity requirements, assess development programs and their potential value, organize technical information for due diligence, and help buyers understand the costs and risks of continued development. By resolving issues early and reducing uncertainty, I aim to facilitate transactions, retain prospective buyers, and improve recoveries.

I provide boutique consulting services to distressed life sciences companies and their boards, investment bankers, restructuring and liquidation advisors, debtors, creditors, assignees, and prospective buyers.

  • Asset assessment and disposition. Inventorying and evaluating therapeutic programs, patents, regulatory filings, clinical data, manufacturing information, and related assets; supporting sale processes and technology transfers. A clear understanding of what is owned, its development status, and its potential uses helps identify assets that might otherwise be overlooked, establish realistic sale expectations, and present opportunities to a broader range of buyers.
  • Patent and regulatory continuity. Tracking patent prosecution deadlines, IND annual reports and other FDA obligations, and coordinating necessary work with patent counsel and regulatory specialists. Timely, targeted action can prevent avoidable lapses in intellectual property rights or regulatory status that could materially reduce an asset's usefulness or sale value. Identifying these obligations early also allows the estate to budget for preservation costs rather than face unexpected expenses.
  • Due diligence and data rooms. Organizing clinical study reports, FDA correspondence, CMC documentation, patent portfolios, contracts, and other records; responding to technical questions from prospective buyers. A navigable data room and prompt, informed responses reduce the effort and uncertainty of evaluating an acquisition, help keep prospective buyers engaged, and may reduce delays, price discounts, or failed transactions.
  • Wind-down operations. Reviewing and maintaining essential vendor arrangements, material storage, regulatory publishing services, databases, and other resources needed to preserve asset value while staff and budgets are reduced. Distinguishing critical continuity costs from services that can be discontinued helps conserve cash without inadvertently losing clinical materials, records, access, or capabilities that a buyer would need.
  • Financial and transaction support. Preparing budgets and cash forecasts, evaluating sale alternatives, and coordinating financial information with management, counsel, and restructuring advisors. Connecting expected preservation and transaction costs with available liquidity helps the estate make timely decisions, compare potential net recoveries rather than headline prices alone, and avoid commitments that cannot be funded.
