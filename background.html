Background

Jonathan Kaufman is an entrepreneur and financial executive with a PhD in Biochemistry and Molecular Biophysics from the University of Pennsylvania and an MBA in Finance from Wharton.

He helped establish five technology companies, including Reaction Biology, Knopp Biosciences, Crystalplex, Immunetrics, and Lipella Pharmaceuticals. He helped start Reaction Biology Corporation, which was sold to Cobepa for a 30X return on investment; was founding CFO of Semprus BioSciences, which raised approximately $30 million in venture capital and was acquired by Teleflex; and co-founded Lipella Pharmaceuticals, which completed a Nasdaq IPO, negotiated a $40 million licensing agreement with Allergan, and advanced therapeutic programs through Phase 2 clinical development.

His experience includes scientific and intellectual property assessment, pharmaceutical development, FDA regulatory affairs, corporate finance, licensing, acquisitions, and the preservation and transfer of biotechnology assets.
