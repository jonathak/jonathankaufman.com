My Background

My professional interests lie at the intersection of science and finance. I have a PhD in Biochemistry and Molecular Biophysics from the University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine and an MBA in Finance from Wharton. Much of my work has involved identifying promising technologies, evaluating their commercial potential, forming and managing companies, and participating in corporate governance.

At LaunchCyte, a biotechnology venture development firm, I sourced and evaluated more than 50 technologies, ultimately leading to the formation of four companies. Reaction Biology was acquired by Cobepa, generating approximately 25 times the original investment. Knopp Biosciences licensed a clinical asset to Biogen, generating approximately nine times the original investment. Crystalplex was acquired by Mojo Vision, and Immunetrics was acquired by Simulations Plus.

I was the founding CFO of Semprus BioSciences, a venture-backed medical technology company, where I managed financial operations, approximately $30 million in venture capital financings, and financial due diligence and closing activities associated with its acquisition by Teleflex.

I also co-founded Lipella Pharmaceuticals, where I was responsible for much of the company's scientific, regulatory, financial, and operational development. Lipella negotiated a $40 million licensing agreement with Allergan, advanced additional therapeutic programs through Phase 2 clinical development, completed a Nasdaq IPO, and ultimately sold its remaining operating assets to XRAIY through a Section 363 sale.

As a Managing Director at Pickwick Capital Partners, I provided corporate finance and transaction advisory services to biotechnology companies, including financial modeling, valuation, financing, and strategic transaction analysis.

Across these activities, I have personally performed much of the specialized work involved in developing, financing, evaluating, and transferring biotechnology assets. This includes technology and intellectual property assessment, patent prosecution and responses to USPTO office actions, university licensing, manufacturing, preclinical and clinical development, IND preparation and maintenance, FDA communications, preclinical and clinical data analysis, business development, licensing, and acquisitions.

I have also managed the preservation and transfer of biotechnology assets in financial distress, including patent prosecution deadlines, FDA reporting obligations, clinical and manufacturing records, specialized vendors, and regulatory sponsorship transfers.

Today, I provide specialized consulting and advisory services to biotechnology companies and their stakeholders in special situations.
