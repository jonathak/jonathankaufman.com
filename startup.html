Startup

Hands-on financial and administrative support for early-stage biotechnology and technology companies, from initial formation through institutional financing. Services range from establishing the company's financial and administrative infrastructure to personally managing its day-to-day financial operations.

Accounting and Financial Reporting

  • Establish and maintain accounting systems and general ledgers.
  • Prepare journal entries, reconcile accounts, and perform monthly closings.
  • Prepare monthly financial statements, budgets, and cash-flow forecasts.
  • Administer vendor and customer transactions, including accounts payable and receivable.
  • Prepare tax returns and annual financial statements.

Corporate Administration

  • Maintain capitalization tables and equity records.
  • Organize corporate records, contracts, and financial documents.
  • Establish and maintain investor due diligence data rooms.
  • Coordinate payroll providers, employee benefits, and administrative vendors.
  • Arrange general liability, workers' compensation, and other corporate insurance coverage.

Financing and Legal Coordination

  • Advise on capitalization and early-stage financing structures.
  • Prepare financial models, capitalization analyses, and investor materials.
  • Prepare schedules, supporting documents, and financial information for corporate and securities counsel.
  • Coordinate financial and corporate due diligence for angel, seed, and venture capital financings.
